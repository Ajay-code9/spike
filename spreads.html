Full transparency on overnight financing, triple swap rollovers, and risk management parameters.

Overnight Rollovers (Swaps)

Positions held open past 21:00 GMT (17:00 New York) are subject to financing swap credits or debits reflecting interbank interest rate differentials.

02

Wednesday 3-Day Triple Swap

To account for weekend settlement when global interbank exchanges are closed, triple swaps are automatically calculated on Wednesday nights for FX & spot metals.

Margin call warning triggers at 50% margin level. Automatic liquidation (Stop Out) activates at 20% to prevent negative account balances.
