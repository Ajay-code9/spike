FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

QUESTIONS ABOUT ACCOUNTS

Everything you need to know about opening, funding, and managing your SPIKE accounts.

Can I open multiple trading accounts under one profile?

Yes. Every verified SPIKE client can open up to 10 sub-accounts in different base currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, USDT) and across different account tiers (e.g. one Standard for swing trading and one Pro ECN for automated scalping).

What is the difference between Standard and Pro ECN?

Standard features simple, all-inclusive spreads from 1.0 pip with zero commission fees, making it ideal for swing and retail traders. Pro ECN delivers true raw interbank pricing starting at 0.0 pips paired with an institutional commission of $3.50 per lot, significantly reducing total transaction costs for high-volume traders.

How does Negative Balance Protection work?

Negative Balance Protection ensures you can never lose more than your total account balance. In extreme black swan market gaps where stop-outs cannot execute, our automated risk bridge immediately resets any negative balance back to zero at our expense.

How do I qualify for VIP volume rebates?

VIP Institutional accounts trading more than 100 round-turn lots per calendar month are automatically enrolled in our volume rebate tiers, unlocking cash rebates of up to 40% on standard commission rates, credited directly to your margin balance weekly.
