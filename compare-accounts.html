FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

ACCOUNT COMPARISON FAQS

Key answers regarding account tier switching, commission models, and execution architecture.

Can I switch between Standard, Pro ECN, and VIP accounts anytime?

Yes. You can switch your account type or open additional sub-accounts directly within the SPIKE Client Portal with a single click. There are no penalty fees or transfer delays when moving funds between your internal accounts.

Why is Pro ECN cheaper overall than Standard despite having commissions?

Standard accounts bundle the broker markup into the spread (starting at 1.0 pip = $10/lot on EUR/USD). Pro ECN passes raw interbank liquidity directly from Tier-1 banks (0.0 to 0.1 pips = ~$1) plus a fixed $3.50 commission per lot, saving up to $5.50 per lot traded.

Are execution speeds and servers identical across all three account tiers?

All SPIKE accounts route through our Equinix NY4 cross-connected trade matching engine with sub-12ms average latency. Pro ECN and VIP tiers receive high-priority queue processing and direct FIX 4.4 bridge connectivity for zero requotes.

Can I test Pro ECN spreads on a risk-free demo account?

Absolutely. You can open a free $50,000 Demo Account that replicates live Pro ECN raw spreads, institutional liquidity depth (Level II DOM), and sub-12ms fill rates across MT5 Desktop, WebTrader, and Mobile apps.
