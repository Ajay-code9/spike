COMPLIANCE & SECURITY

Accepted Verification Documents Checklist

To comply with international regulatory standards and AML regulations, please prepare clear digital photos of the following documents.

Proof of Identity (POI)
  • International Passport (Recommended)
  • Government-Issued National Identity Card
  • Valid Full Driver's License
  • Must clearly show full legal name & photo
  • Must show date of birth & document expiry
  • All 4 corners of document must be visible
Proof of Residence (POA)
  • Utility Bill (Electricity, Water, Gas, Internet)
  • Certified Bank or Credit Card Statement
  • Local Council Tax or Government Assessment
  • Must be issued within the last 90 days
  • Must show your full name & residential address
  • Screenshots or cropped files are not accepted
Corporate Entity Documents
  • Certificate of Incorporation
  • Memorandum & Articles of Association
  • Register of Directors and Shareholders
  • Proof of registered corporate address
  • POI & POA for beneficial owners (>25% equity)
  • Resolution of Directors to open account

ONBOARDING & VERIFICATION FAQ

Key details to help you get your account verified and funded quickly.

How long does automated KYC verification take?

Our automated AI verification system approves 95% of standard applications within 15 minutes. In cases where manual document review is required, our compliance desk verifies documents within 2 to 4 business hours.

Can I fund my account before completing verification?

You can deposit up to $2,000 immediately upon basic email confirmation to begin exploring platforms and placing orders. However, full KYC verification is mandatory before executing withdrawals or unlocking higher leverage tiers.

Are my deposited funds protected in segregated accounts?

Yes, 100%. All client funds are held in segregated Tier-1 bank accounts completely separate from SPIKE's operational capital. Funds cannot be used by the firm for hedging, liquidity provider operations, or operational expenses under any circumstances.

What is the minimum initial deposit to activate an account?

The minimum deposit is $100 for a Standard Account, $1,000 for a Pro ECN Account, and $10,000 for a VIP Institutional Account. You can also start with a $0 risk-free demo account loaded with $50,000 in virtual funds at any time.
