E EXECUTION

ECN (Electronic Communication Network)

An automated bridge that connects market participants directly to tier-1 institutional liquidity providers (banks, hedge funds, and prime brokers) with zero dealing desk intervention.

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

When you place a 1.00 lot EUR/USD buy order, the ECN router matches your order with the lowest available interbank ask price instantly without requotes.

#STP #RawSpreads #NoDealingDesk
S EXECUTION

STP (Straight-Through Processing)

An electronic routing system where client orders are passed straight through to liquidity providers for instant filling without manual re-handling or order modifications.

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

Trades are routed in sub-12 milliseconds directly into our Equinix NY4 server farm, eliminating dealer intervention entirely.

#NDD #DirectRouting #Latency
S EXECUTION

Slippage (Positive & Negative)

The difference between the expected execution price of an order and the actual filled price during high volatility or low liquidity market conditions.

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

You click Buy GBP/USD at 1.25000 during NFP release. The order fills at 1.25004 (-0.4 pips negative slippage) or 1.24996 (+0.4 pips positive price improvement).

#Volatility #OrderFilling #NewsEvents
P FOREX MECHANICS

Pip & Pipette

The standardized unit of measure representing the smallest fractional price movement in currency exchange rates. 1 Pip = 0.0001 (or 0.01 in JPY pairs). A Pipette is 1/10th of a pip.

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

EUR/USD moves from 1.08500 to 1.08550 = 5.0 pips gained. On a 1.00 standard lot trade, 1 pip equals approximately $10.00 USD.

#LotSize #PointValue #PriceCalculation
B FOREX MECHANICS

Bid-Ask Spread

The spread is the difference between the Bid price (the highest price a buyer will pay) and the Ask price (the lowest price a seller will accept).

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

XAU/USD (Gold) is quoted Bid 2,400.10 / Ask 2,400.22. The spread is 1.2 pips ($0.12). A tight spread minimizes your breakeven cost.

#RawPricing #TransactionCost #Gold
L FOREX MECHANICS

Leverage & Required Margin

Leverage allows traders to control large position sizes using a smaller amount of committed collateral known as Required Margin.

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

With 1:500 leverage, opening a $100,000 EUR/USD position requires only $200 in initial margin deposit ($100,000 ÷ 500 = $200).

#MarginRatio #PurchasingPower #Risk
L FOREX MECHANICS

Lot Size (Standard, Mini, Micro)

Standardized contract units used for trade execution: 1 Standard Lot = 100,000 units, 1 Mini Lot (0.10) = 10,000 units, 1 Micro Lot (0.01) = 1,000 units.

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

A micro lot (0.01) on EUR/USD moves approximately $0.10 per pip, allowing precision risk control for smaller starting accounts.

#ContractUnits #Volume #PositionSizing
S FOREX MECHANICS

Swap & Overnight Rollover

The interest differential fee paid or earned when holding open leveraged positions past the global market close time (22:00 GMT).

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

If you buy a currency with a 5% interest rate against one with 1%, you earn positive net swap daily. Spike offers 100% Swap-Free Islamic accounts.

#InterestRate #IslamicAccount #Rollover
M RISK MANAGEMENT

Margin Call & Stop Out Level

A Margin Call alerts you when equity drops to 100% of required margin. Stop Out triggers automated liquidation (at 50% margin level) to prevent account depletion.

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

If your required margin is $500 and floating losses drop equity to $250 (50%), the automated risk engine closes the largest losing trade first.

#CapitalSafety #Liquidation #MarginLevel
R RISK MANAGEMENT

Risk-to-Reward Ratio (R:R)

The mathematical comparison between the capital risked on a trade (Stop Loss) versus the expected profit potential (Take Profit).

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

Risking $100 to make $300 represents a 1:3 R:R. A trader with a 1:3 ratio can be profitable with only a 35% win rate over 100 trades.

#MathEdge #StopLoss #TakeProfit
M RISK MANAGEMENT

Maximum Drawdown (MDD)

The peak-to-trough decline during a specific record period of an investment portfolio or trading strategy, expressed as a percentage of peak capital.

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

An account grows from $10,000 to $15,000, then dips to $12,000 before rising again. The drawdown is $3,000 / $15,000 = 20% MDD.

#PortfolioRisk #QuantAnalytics #PropTrading
H RISK MANAGEMENT

Hedging & Netting Accounts

Hedging allows opening simultaneous Long and Short positions in the same instrument. Netting consolidates opposite orders into a single net market position.

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

On MT5 Hedging accounts, you can buy 1 lot EUR/USD and simultaneously sell 1 lot EUR/USD to lock in profits or neutralize market exposure during news.

#MT5Features #LockPositions #RiskMitigation
D TECHNICAL & DOM

Depth of Market (Level II DOM)

A real-time window showing cumulative order liquidity available across multiple bid and ask price tiers beyond the top-of-book best quotes.

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

Our Level II DOM ladder displays up to 10 pricing levels so institutional quants can evaluate order book imbalances before submitting high-volume orders.

#OrderBook #Level2 #LiquidityVolume
F TECHNICAL & DOM

Fair Value Gap (FVG)

A three-candle price action pattern formed by aggressive institutional buying or selling where the wicks of candle 1 and candle 3 do not overlap, leaving an inefficiency.

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

Institutional traders wait for price to retrace back into the unfilled FVG zone to enter high-confluence limit orders at discount prices.

#SmartMoney #Imbalance #PriceAction
O TECHNICAL & DOM

Order Flow & Liquidity Pools

Analyzing the execution velocity and resting clusters of stop-loss orders situated above key swing highs and below swing lows where institutional participants look to fill size.

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

A sweep of equal highs captures retail buy stop orders, providing liquidity for institutional market participants to execute short blocks.

#VolumeProfile #LiquiditySweep #SmartMoney
C TECHNICAL & DOM

Candlestick Rejection Wick

An elongated shadow (wick) on a candlestick that indicates an aggressive intra-period reversal where buyers or sellers swiftly rejected an extreme price level.

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

A 4-hour Gold candle drops into $2,380 support but closes near $2,400 with a long bottom wick, signaling strong institutional absorption.

#PinBar #PriceAction #Absorption
F MACRO & CENTRAL BANKS

FOMC (Federal Open Market Committee)

The policy-making body of the U.S. Federal Reserve responsible for setting the Fed Funds benchmark interest rate and directing national monetary policy.

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

When the FOMC announces an unexpected 25bps rate hike, the US Dollar (USD) typically surges across major pairs due to higher yield appeal.

#FederalReserve #USD #MonetaryPolicy
N MACRO & CENTRAL BANKS

Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP)

A key U.S. employment metric released on the first Friday of every month measuring the change in total employed workers, excluding farm, government, and non-profit jobs.

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

An NFP print coming in at +275K vs +180K expected causes intense immediate volatility across Gold, US Indices, and EUR/USD.

#EconomicCalendar #Employment #Volatility
C MACRO & CENTRAL BANKS

Consumer Price Index (CPI)

A headline economic indicator tracking the average change over time in the prices paid by urban consumers for a market basket of consumer goods and services (inflation).

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

Higher-than-expected CPI reports fuel market bets on tighter central bank interest rates, driving bond yields and domestic currency valuations.

#Inflation #CentralBank #MacroData

A EXECUTION

Arbitrage (Latency & Triangular)

The simultaneous purchase and sale of an identical or equivalent financial asset across different liquidity venues or currency triangles to capitalize on fleeting pricing discrepancies.

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

Institutional quants identify EUR/USD at 1.08500 in London and 1.08504 in NY4, executing micro-second simultaneous fills to capture the spread.

#Algorithms #PriceDiscrepancy #HFT
H MACRO & CENTRAL BANKS

Hawkish vs. Dovish Monetary Stance

'Hawkish' refers to central bankers prioritizing inflation control by raising interest rates. 'Dovish' refers to prioritizing economic growth and employment by lowering rates.

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

A hawkish ECB press conference signals impending rate hikes, sparking institutional capital inflows into EUR pairs.

#ECB #RatePolicy #MacroRadar
Y MACRO & CENTRAL BANKS

Yield Curve & Inversion

A graphical curve plotting interest rates of sovereign bonds across differing maturities. An inversion (2-year yield higher than 10-year yield) historically precedes economic recessions.

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

When the US 2Y/10Y yield curve inverts, defensive capital flows aggressively into safe-haven assets including Gold (XAU) and Swiss Franc (CHF).

#BondYields #RecessionSignal #SafeHaven
T RISK MANAGEMENT

Trailing Stop Loss Order

A dynamic stop-loss order that automatically tracks market price at a specified distance when the market moves in favor of your trade, locking in unrealized gains.

PRACTICAL SCENARIO

You buy Gold at $2,400 with a 30-pip trailing stop. As Gold climbs to $2,420, your stop moves automatically to $2,417, guaranteeing locked-in profit.

#TradeManagement #ProfitLock #Automation
